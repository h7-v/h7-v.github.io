I used to thoroughly enjoy the Christmas period but after some loss and grief over the past 5 or so years, I find myself just checking out of it all. Not to mention that I'm the slightly awkward one at family meetups, so I spent much of the past few days alone resting, which led to some thoughts (oh no).

So what's the deal? I've been working hard on something, alone, in my own time and unpaid, for a year. It's not strict, but I'm under a self-imposed NDA of sorts to not leak anything. This text right here is the first time any information about it is finding its way onto the internet. I have been designing a product for my employer - a powerful sequel to an existing product from the early 70s that seized production in that same decade. We are now 54 years on. Prototype "v0.1" is complete and working, meaning that this project has turned into something with actual commercial viability.

I'm not just messing around with a toy project anymore here, and we're four figures deep in development costs, obviously not including the time I've spent in R&D as mentioned. The funding for the project came fairly early on when I only had some very primitive functionality to show, and my boss was kind enough based on that demonstration to start investing in the project and in my own belief that I could pull this off.

This is the first time I'm being depended on to deliver a real, commercial product, and although I haven't been thinking about it like that up to this point, I suppose it hit home after v0.1 reached completion and the holiday season began.

So now I'm in the trenches... The most important functionality of the device is present, but now the novelty has worn off, and the late nights are starting to become more gruelling. Interface design, scaling back anxiously implemented over-engineered elements, documentation, bug fixes (firmware AND hardware), making drivers more robust etc. I have a long list of things to do, most of which are easy enough, although some are much more complex and mind-bending.

I think the point of this post is to try and get off my chest just how unexpectedly lonely this process has turned out to be. My colleagues are exclusively analogue guys and my close family and friends are not embedded engineers. It feels as if I have nobody to share the struggle with and nobody to confide in. I'm down here in the mud with a very long path ahead. I've never experienced this before, and it's harder than I thought it would be.

People have been waiting for this product for years. The stakes are high, there is third party investment and there *are* deadlines albeit self-imposed.

All of this, and I still have a floordrobe.

Godspeed to me I guess
