I think I'm in the good old days right now. Despite the stresses that come with the project I'm working on, my overall quality of life is relatively high. I've definitely been in positions within the last 5 years that have felt like hell in general at the time, but I look back at it somewhat fondly now. At the very end of 2022 onto 23 a couple of my friends had me come online to play WoW Dragonflight on release with them, complete with the solo grind of starting a new character in the game from scratch. I usually feel fairly iffy about playing games that friends recommend because I don't really play games anymore, but I briefly played WoW during Cataclysm when I was quite young and thought it would probably be worth giving it a go.

At the time, I was working at a recording studio on and off while working for my current employer a few days per week. The studio work was intensely stressful and not something I wish to return to, but coming home to log on with my friends each evening to play and gear up our characters with the best items in the game at the time are memories I'm very nostalgic about. I've historically always played bow classes in RPG games, so I levelled a Hunter just like I did back in Cataclysm. On top of this, the final boss of the first raid of Dragonflight, Raszageth, dropped a bow that was completely broken on release, "Neltharax, Enemy of the Sky." At the time, it was easily the strongest item in the game, regardless of class. Every session I played, I tried to better my character just so I could get as many opportunities as possible to get into raids that would allow me to get the best version of Neltharax possible. I was totally obsessed with that bow and working towards getting it.

I'm also totally obsessed with what I'm building and working towards finishing it right now. I will almost certainly cherish and feel nostalgic about the late nights I'm pulling and the small wins I'm achieving as this thing comes to life.
